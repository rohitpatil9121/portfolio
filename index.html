Document Intelligence Pipeline

2026

Drop a PDF, an image or a recording into S3 and EventBridge starts a Step Functions workflow: Textract or Transcribe pulls out the content, Bedrock summarises it, and Titan turns it into embeddings stored in S3 Vectors. You can then search the whole corpus semantically — including phrases spoken inside an audio file. Failures land in an SQS dead-letter queue with a replay script.
